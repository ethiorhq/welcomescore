# Governance

## Purpose

WelcomeScore is an ETHIOR project that helps maintainers identify public signals that make repositories more approachable for first-time contributors. Governance exists to protect that purpose, keep changes reviewable, and maintain a respectful, secure project environment.

## Stewardship

ETHIOR is the project steward and copyright owner. The steward retains final authority over the project name, releases, production deployments, trademarks/brand identifiers, licensing, policy interpretation, and acceptance of contributions.

Maintainers may be delegated practical responsibilities such as issue triage, pull-request review, documentation improvements, and release preparation. Delegation does not transfer ownership, create an employment relationship, or authorize a maintainer to bind ETHIOR legally.

## Decision principles

Project decisions should prioritize:

1. **Evidence-bound scoring.** Repository scores and Algofox guidance must rely only on documented, verifiable public contributor signals.
2. **User control.** Hall of Fame additions, reviews, sharing, Dev Lounge posts, reactions, and other state-changing actions must remain explicit user choices.
3. **Privacy and safety.** Secrets remain server-side; users should not be asked to publish sensitive information; and security reports remain private.
4. **Accessibility and restraint.** The product should remain understandable, responsive, keyboard-accessible, respectful of reduced motion, and free of manipulative visual or behavioral patterns.
5. **Maintainability.** Prefer small, documented, validated changes over broad rewrites or duplicated logic.
6. **Truthful attribution.** Preserve copyright, license, and original-project attribution in this repository and permitted derivatives.

## Change process

Material code, policy, scoring, data, or deployment changes should be proposed through a focused issue or pull request, reviewed for scope and user impact, validated locally, and recorded through Git history. Security fixes may use a private process under `SECURITY.md` before a public release.

Changes that introduce new data collection, third-party processing, server-side secrets, user-generated-content flows, automated actions, or policy obligations require explicit review of their privacy, safety, and operational impact.

## Policy updates

ETHIOR may update this document and related policies as the product changes. Material policy updates should be published through the repository and reflected on the relevant public policy page. Continued use of the hosted service after a published update is governed by the updated terms to the extent permitted by law.

## Community enforcement

The Code of Conduct and project terms are enforced according to their published procedures. Maintainers may act promptly to protect users, project infrastructure, or the integrity of public information. Enforcement decisions are project-governance decisions and are not legal determinations.
