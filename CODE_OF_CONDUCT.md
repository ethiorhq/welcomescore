# WelcomeScore Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

WelcomeScore aims to make first contributions easier. We expect project discussions, pull requests, issues, reviews, and Dev Lounge participation to be practical, respectful, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participation in this repository and its associated community spaces is governed by this Code of Conduct. Maintainers will make good-faith efforts to apply it consistently and proportionately.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and community participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience, clarity, and respect;
- focus feedback on code, documentation, product behavior, and ideas rather than personal traits or identity;
- welcome newcomers and explain project context without gatekeeping;
- give credit for work, respect licensing and attribution requirements, and disclose conflicts of interest where relevant;
- protect security, privacy, and confidentiality by avoiding credentials, personal data, private repository content, and vulnerability details in public spaces;
- follow platform rules, applicable law, and the project’s published policies; and
- disengage from a discussion when asked by a maintainer or when the discussion is no longer constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project-controlled spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, discrimination, hateful conduct, or sexualized content;
- personal attacks, insults, deliberate humiliation, dogpiling, or repeated bad-faith disruption;
- publishing another person’s private or identifying information without permission;
- sharing passwords, tokens, private keys, security vulnerabilities, or private communications without authorization;
- spam, scams, phishing, impersonation, deceptive links, malware, unsolicited commercial promotion, or attempts to move users into unsafe private deals;
- soliciting payment, investment, employment, services, or confidential information through the Dev Lounge or project channels;
- misrepresenting project affiliation, ownership, endorsement, audit results, or the origin of derivative work; or
- bypassing platform controls or attempting to interfere with project infrastructure, community safety, or other participants.

## Scope

This Code applies in the source repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, releases, documentation, the WelcomeScore Dev Lounge, and official project-controlled spaces. It may also apply to public conduct outside those spaces when that conduct has a direct and material impact on the safety of the WelcomeScore community.

## Reporting a concern

For urgent safety, credible threats, security vulnerabilities, or unlawful activity, contact the relevant platform or local emergency service first where appropriate. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

For project conduct concerns, use the private reporting option available through the canonical [WelcomeScore repository](https://github.com/ethiorhq/welcomescore) or contact a project maintainer through that repository. Include only the information needed to assess the concern: links, dates, relevant content, and a safe way to follow up. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, but absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where disclosure is necessary to investigate, comply with law, or protect people.

Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.

## Enforcement process

Maintainers may review the reported material, seek limited clarification, preserve necessary records, and consult an appropriate advisor where needed. Responses are context-dependent and may include a private reminder, public clarification, content removal, temporary restriction, rejection of a contribution, suspension from a project space, or a permanent ban. Serious threats, security issues, fraud, or unlawful conduct may be escalated to a platform provider or relevant authority where appropriate.

An enforcement decision is not a finding of legal liability. ETHIOR may update, interpret, or enforce this Code to protect the project and its community.

## Appeals

A person directly affected by an enforcement decision may request a good-faith reconsideration through the same private project channel, explaining the factual basis for the request. An appeal does not guarantee a different outcome and does not pause a safety measure unless a maintainer confirms that it does.

## Acknowledgment

By participating, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct. This document does not create employment, partnership, agency, fiduciary, or contractual rights beyond those that applicable law requires or that a separately signed agreement creates.
