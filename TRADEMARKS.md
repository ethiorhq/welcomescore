# Trademark and Brand Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains the limited permitted use of ETHIOR and WelcomeScore brand identifiers. It complements the project `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and `ATTRIBUTION.md`. It does not replace the source-available license or grant rights beyond those documents.

## Brand identifiers

The following may be trademarks, service marks, trade names, or brand identifiers of ETHIOR: **ETHIOR**, **WelcomeScore**, **WelcomeScore.js.org**, associated logos, product names, domain names, and visual identity elements.

## Permitted use without separate permission

You may use the names and links solely to provide accurate Required Attribution under the project license, to identify the unmodified original project, or to make a truthful factual reference to the canonical project. For example:

> Original project: WelcomeScore.js.org by ETHIOR.

You may also state that your work is derived from WelcomeScore if that statement is accurate and if you clearly identify your modifications.

## Uses requiring written permission

Do not use ETHIOR or WelcomeScore branding in a way that suggests sponsorship, affiliation, certification, approval, partnership, or operation by ETHIOR without prior written permission. This includes using the original logos as your own product identity, registering confusingly similar domains or social accounts, representing a fork as official, or using an audit score as a commercial endorsement.

## Derivatives and public deployments

A derivative may use its own name and brand. It must retain the Required Attribution, notices, and links required by the source-available license. Visible attribution is an honest source notice; it does not grant a right to present a derivative as the canonical WelcomeScore service.

## Reporting suspected misuse

Report suspected misleading use through the canonical [WelcomeScore repository](https://github.com/ethiorhq/welcomescore). Include public links and a concise explanation. Do not make public accusations or share sensitive personal information.

## No technical lockout

This project does not use hidden code or destructive failure behavior to enforce attribution. Attribution obligations are transparent and documented through the license, notices, and this policy. This approach avoids harming legitimate users of a modified deployment while preserving ETHIOR’s stated rights.
