# Attribution Guide

WelcomeScore is created and maintained by **ETHIOR**. This guide explains the attribution requirement in the project’s source-available license in practical terms.

## Canonical attribution

For any public redistribution, hosted deployment, public demo, or derivative repository, retain a visible credit substantially equivalent to:

> Original project: [WelcomeScore.js.org](https://welcomescore.vercel.app) · Source: [github.com/ethiorhq/welcomescore](https://github.com/ethiorhq/welcomescore)

For a website, place this credit in the footer or in a clearly linked Credits/About page reachable from every public page. The credit must remain legible, functional where links are supported, and must not be hidden behind a sign-in wall or visually obscured.

## Modifications and branding

You may add your own branding, name your own product, and describe your own modifications. Make the relationship accurate. A suitable form is:

> Built on WelcomeScore by ETHIOR. Modified by [Your Organization].

Do not state or imply that ETHIOR endorses, operates, certifies, sponsors, or supports your modified version unless ETHIOR has agreed in writing.

## Repository notice

A derivative repository should retain `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` and add a short statement near the top of its README, for example:

```md
This project is derived from [WelcomeScore](https://github.com/ethiorhq/welcomescore) by ETHIOR and includes local modifications.
```

## Why this matters

Attribution gives users a truthful source trail, helps them find the original documentation and security information, and recognizes the work that made the derivative possible. The exact legal obligations are in `LICENSE`; this guide is a practical explanation, not a replacement for the license.
